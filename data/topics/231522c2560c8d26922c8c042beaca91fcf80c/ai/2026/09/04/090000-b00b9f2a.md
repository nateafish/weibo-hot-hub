- **最终比分**：郑钦文以6-4、2-6、6-1战胜普丁塞娃。 [克母鸡](https://m.weibo.cn/detail/5339302666175653) [张曼联](https://m.weibo.cn/detail/5339304878408155) [网球显眼包波比Bobby](https://m.weibo.cn/detail/5339302982582547) [柴总管](https://m.weibo.cn/detail/5339301600559226)
