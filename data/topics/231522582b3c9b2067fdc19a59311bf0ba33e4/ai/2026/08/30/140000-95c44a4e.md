- **馈赠不收回**：栾念在六年恋情中陆续赠送尚之桃14个高奢品牌包，大部分为限量款。分手时他未作任何追讨，全部留在女方手中。 [星冷熬](https://m.weibo.cn/detail/5337597941651531) [闻汤识面泡剧](https://m.weibo.cn/detail/5337561331663587) [兔牙肫](https://m.weibo.cn/detail/5337551516991988) [涂染海棠红](https://m.weibo.cn/detail/5337604964749052) [绒兔雨](https://m.weibo.cn/detail/5337553213590776) [紫薯不打烊](https://m.weibo.cn/detail/5337541767333001) [娱乐揽星河](https://m.weibo.cn/detail/5337550363298408) [爱吃玉米的果冻猫](https://m.weibo.cn/detail/5337538558691822) [在逃娱公主](https://m.weibo.cn/detail/5336458290530458) [Windfall茜](https://m.weibo.cn/detail/5337561431540570) [圈内指南](https://m.weibo.cn/detail/5337565881173369) [脸红的尸春期](https://m.weibo.cn/detail/5334900118913698) [魔芋专业选手](https://m.weibo.cn/detail/5337561728024968) [我萌宅](https://m.weibo.cn/detail/5337336868503870)
